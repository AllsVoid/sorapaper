Soft interfaces work best when they are restrained. Rounded cards, gentle gradients and blur can create atmosphere, but they need strong spacing and contrast to stay usable.

## What Matters

The most important details are usually quiet:

1. Consistent corner radius.
2. Low saturation accents.
3. Shadows that suggest depth without floating away.
4. Text contrast that remains readable in both light and dark modes.

## A Small Rule

If an effect competes with the title or paragraph text, it should be reduced.
